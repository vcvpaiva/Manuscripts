### Notes not intended for journals

Lab notes, recording calculations for the record. [blog post on lab notes](https://logic-forall.blogspot.com/2026/10/lab-notes-what-and-why.html)
* [Dialectica categories without subobjects](https://github.com/vcvpaiva/Manuscripts/blob/main/DialWithoutSubobjects2.pdf), 2026, just for the record: can do Dial both with subobjects and with maps into a `lineale' L.
* [Dialectica Constructions over Monoidal Bases](https://github.com/vcvpaiva/Manuscripts/blob/main/DialOverMonoidal.pdf), 2026, for the record: can do Dial over a smcc with products, just like can do Gir with the same assumptions (technical report on multirelations, TR225). A sensible example is graded sets.
* [Dialectica over a Topos](https://github.com/vcvpaiva/Manuscripts/blob/main/DialOverTopos.pdf), Nov 2026, very much what you'd expect. Also, Hyland's trick for Sets (thesis page 39) stops working.
* [Factorization Systems for Dialectica Categories](https://github.com/vcvpaiva/Manuscripts/blob/main/factorization-dialectica.pdf), Nov 2026, for the record: factorization systems on the base category C lift to Gir and, with more care, to Dial.
